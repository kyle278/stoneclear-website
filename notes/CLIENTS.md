## Responsive requirement

Kyle requested no emojis on mobile or tablet. Active site text and controls use words and Lucide SVG icons at all sizes. The logo rail has no autoplay; native touch/trackpad scrolling, keyboard scrolling and labelled previous/next buttons expose the logos. The full Clients page is a responsive grid, with plain names for unsourced marks.
