# Design Context

## Users
Stone Clear homeowners and commercial/hospitality property managers in Carlow, Dublin, Kildare and across Ireland. They need to inspect authentic restoration outcomes and arrange a demonstration or quote.

## Brand Personality
Careful, practical, confident. Preserve the existing Stone Clear fan motif and recognisable lettering; use the site's Clear blue #1258a4 as requested by Kyle.

## Aesthetic Direction
Existing approved light architectural composition. Kyle chose Expressive motion. Current refinement requests: layered draggable before/after reveal with back-and-forth animation, monochrome vector logo without tagline, and primary font closely matched to the logo with a complementary secondary face.

## Design Principles
- Real workmanship and honest captions lead; source images can differ in viewpoint.
- Motion explains comparison and yields immediately to manual control.
- Respect system and site reduced-motion preferences, offscreen and hidden-tab performance.
- Vector mark uses no embedded raster; primary typography must fit the existing identity.
- Maintain readable layouts, keyboard access and the verified contact journey.
